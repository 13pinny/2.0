Stock

A link fills in the name, venue and date where the site allows it (AXS blocks this, so only the name comes from its link). Type straight into the grid: Enter moves down, Tab moves right, and every cell saves on its own. The yellow column is today.

Add shows from any ticket page

Ticketmaster and AXS block Kartis's server, but they load fine in your own browser. This bookmark reads the show's name, date, venue, city and artwork from the page you're on and adds it here. If the show is already on /stock, it fills in whatever is missing.

Computer: drag this button to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any event page.

+ Kartis Stock

Phone: bookmark any page, edit that bookmark, and paste this in as its address. Then open the bookmark while you're on the event page.

Ticketmaster tickets left: the Kartis Stock Chrome extension does what the bookmark does and also fills today's tickets left and price per section from Ticketmaster's own seat-map data. To install it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the chrome_extension\kartis_stock folder in the Kartis repo. Then open (or reload) a Ticketmaster event, let the seat map finish loading, and click the extension's button.

Import a Google Sheet

Paste the sheet's share link (it must be shared as "anyone with the link"), or paste the rows themselves below. A show that's already here gets the new numbers merged in, not duplicated.
