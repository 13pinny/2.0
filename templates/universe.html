Counts come straight from Universe (Ticketmaster): released, left and sold per tier, prices all-in. Tiers open one after another; a "Next" tier opens when the one before it sells out, and its count isn't in "Left now". Pings: a tier coming back in stock and price drops go to #universe-shocks, the price stepping up to the next tier and your "under N left" alert to #jumps, sell-outs to #status, new tiers to new events.
