KUPAT · PRINT TICKETS TO PDF

Paste a Kupat e-document URL (the link from your purchase email of the form tickets.kupat.co.il/edocument/<uuid>/?id=<number>) and we’ll drive a headless browser to render each ticket in the order as its own PDF, named <EventName>_<Date>_<Section>_Row<N>_Seat<N>.pdf, saved into a per-event folder under $KARTIS_TICKETS_DIR (defaults to ~/OneDrive/KartisTickets/). Takes ~5s per ticket.

TICKCHAK · REDACT + SPLIT TICKETS

Upload a multi-page Tickchak ticket PDF. We strip the buyer name, phone, ticket price, and Tickchak logo from every page, then split it into one PDF per ticket — saved into the same per-event folder under $KARTIS_TICKETS_DIR as the Kupat tool, named <EventName>_<Date>_<Section>_Row<N>_Seat<N>.pdf.

PACHA NY · FETCH TICKETS FROM GMAIL

Scans the dashboard’s Gmail inbox for Pacha New York / FourVenues ticket emails (forward them to fwd.pbtsign@gmail.com), downloads each ticket PDF, and keeps page 1 of each — grouped per event and numbered, e.g. Pacha ticket 1 of 10 - Alok.pdf. The tickets are bundled into a zip you download in your browser (de-duped, totals self-correct).
