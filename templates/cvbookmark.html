Then, whenever you want your sales up to date:

  1. Be signed into crowdvolt.com in this browser — your normal one, no special window.
  2. Be on a crowdvolt.com page.
  3. Click the bookmark. A box appears in the corner and tells you what landed.

Why it lives here and not on the dashboard

Your CrowdVolt session cookie is HttpOnly and scoped to crowdvolt.com. A button on this site cannot read it, and this server cannot reach into your browser. Running from a crowdvolt.com page is what lets your own logged-in session do the reading — no token to harvest, no second Chrome, no agent.

The bookmark carries a token that authorises the import. Treat it like a password: anyone holding it could file fake sales rows into your ledger. If it leaks, change KARTIS_CVSALES_TOKEN on the box, reload this page and drag the link again. It cannot touch your CrowdVolt session or anything else on kartis.
