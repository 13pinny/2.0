Why there are no ticket counts here. Tao publishes none — the quantity dropdown only reveals an unpublished per-order limit, so "N of M left" and units-sold-per-hour don't exist for these rooms. What Tao does publish is the numbered GA ladder, and a tier only steps up once the one below it sells out. So burn time — how long each release lasted — is the sales signal, and the price climb is the demand curve. Pings for these events already go to #jumps (price climbs) and #shocks (price drops, restocks).
